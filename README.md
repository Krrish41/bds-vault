## Central Hub Capabilities & Features

1. **Executive Study Cockpit**:
   - Live dashboard reading real-time syllabus completion from `localStorage` across all 3 portals.
   - Dynamic multi-segment progress bar reflecting mastery across all 66 curriculum topics (63 comprehensive university essays + 3 master rapid revision cheat sheets).
2. **Dynamic 3-Paper 15-Mark University Marking Rubric**:
   - Interactive segmented switcher (`Surgery` / `Medicine` / `Pathology`) dynamically rendering subject-specific 10-tier examiner scoring criteria.
3. **Chairside Clinical Emergency Cutoffs & Lab Norms**:
   - Quick-reference modal and side-drawer displaying vital hematological and physiological cutoffs: Platelet counts for extractions ($> 50,000/\mu\text{L}$), PT/INR safe thresholds ($\le 2.5 - 3.0$), Fasting glucose ($< 180\text{ mg/dL}$), and severe hypertension alerts ($> 180/110\text{ mmHg}$).
4. **Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`)**:
   - Instant fuzzy search across all 66 curriculum topics, 40 microscopic spotters, and clinical cutoffs with direct deep-links.
5. **Integrated Focus Timer**:
   - Built-in Pomodoro study dock (25m / 50m presets) with gentle audio chime notifications.
6. **Ecosystem Parity & Standing Architecture**:
   - Automated SHA256 byte-for-byte synchronization engine (`sync_portal_core.py`) maintaining design tokens, core-shell CSS, and engine JavaScript across all 4 repositories.
7. **Color-Calibrated Specialty Identities**:
   - **Paper I**: Clinical Surgical Teal (`#14b8a6`) with scalpel vector and surgical grid watermark.
   - **Paper II**: Diagnostic Clinical Indigo (`#6366f1`) with stethoscope vector and ECG pulse watermark.
   - **Paper III**: Histological Magenta (`#d946ef`) with microscope vector and tissue stroma watermark.
   - **Hub**: Obsidian luxury slate (`#090a12`, `#101322`, `#161a2e`) with zero white flash.

## 3rd Year Clinical Foundations Curriculum (66 Curriculum Topics)

### Paper I: General Surgery (24 Chapters: 23 Essays + Rapid Revision Sheet)
- **Core Principles**: Shock resuscitation, Blood transfusion & TRALI/TACO, Surgical infections & necrotizing fasciitis, Hilton's I&D method, Surgical site infections (CDC), Gas gangrene & Tetanus.
- **Head & Neck**: 10-Neck swellings matrix, Cervical lymphadenopathy (Tubercular cold abscess), Wound healing & keloids, Diathermy (monopolar vs bipolar), Maxillofacial trauma (Mandible & Le Fort I, II, III).
- **Specialty Surgery**: Abdominal TB, Actinomycosis, Trigeminal neuralgia, Head injury & intracranial hematomas (EDH vs SDH), Theater sterilization & zoning, Sutures & Needles, Drains & Tubes.
- **Oncology & Tech**: Lasers, Cryosurgery, Anesthesia principles, Skin grafts (STSG vs FTSG), Thyroid gland neoplasms & Thyroidectomy.
- **Rapid Revision**: Master Surgery Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet (High-yield matrices, formulas, scores, and exam night pearls).

### Paper II: General Medicine (21 Chapters: 20 Essays + Rapid Revision Sheet)
- **Hepatology & GI**: Acute viral hepatitis serology, Acute liver failure & hepatic encephalopathy, Cirrhosis & Portal HTN, PEM (Kwashiorkor vs Marasmus), Dysphagia & Peptic ulcer disease.
- **Cardiovascular**: Acute rheumatic fever & Jones criteria, Infective endocarditis & AHA dental prophylaxis, Ischemic heart disease & STEMI MONA-B, Heart failure & Systemic hypertension, Congenital heart diseases.
- **Infections & Exanthems**: Enteric fever (Typhoid BASU), Syphilis (primary to tertiary), Diphtheria & Tetanus, Viral exanthems & HIV/AIDS oral manifestations.
- **Endocrine & Hematology**: Diabetes mellitus & DKA, The Leukemias (AML, ALL, CML, CLL), Nephrotic vs Nephritic syndrome & CKD.
- **Neurology & Emergencies**: Facial nerve paralysis (Bell's palsy), Acute meningitis CSF analysis, Shock syndromes & ACLS CPR algorithm.
- **Rapid Revision**: Master Medicine Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet (Bedside scoring algorithms, emergency drug doses, and chairside medical protocols).

### Paper III: Oral Pathology & Microbiology (21 Chapters: 20 Essays + Rapid Revision Sheet + 40 Spotters)
- **Odontogenic Neoplasms & Cysts**: Ameloblastoma & Vickers-Gorlin criteria, CEOT / Pindborg tumor, AOT, Odontomas, OKC / KCOT & Gorlin-Goltz syndrome, Dentigerous & radicular cysts, Developmental non-odontogenic cysts.
- **Salivary & Premalignancy**: Pleomorphic adenoma, Warthin's tumor, Mucoepidermoid & Adenoid cystic carcinoma, Mucoceles & Sialolithiasis, Leukoplakia & Erythroplakia (OPMD), OSMF & Oral Lichen Planus, Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC), Soft tissue mesenchymal tumors.
- **Caries & Developmental**: Dental caries histopathology (enamel & dentin zones), Tooth structural anomalies (AI, DI, DD), Number/shape (Anodontia, Gemination, Fusion), Craniofacial anomalies (Cleft lip/palate, Lingual thyroid).
- **Infections, Bone & Forensic**: Oral TB, Syphilis, Actinomycosis, Candidiasis, HSV-1 & Tzanck smear, Vesiculobullous diseases (Pemphigus vs Pemphigoid), Bone pathology (Fibrous dysplasia, Paget's disease), Gustafson's age estimation & cheiloscopy.
- **Rapid Revision & Spotters**: Master Oral Pathology Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet (Pathognomonic buzzwords, cyst/tumor matrices, syndromic associations) & 40 Microscopic Spotters.
