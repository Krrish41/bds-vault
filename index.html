Normal Clinical Laboratory Values (Chairside Reference)

Parameter Standard Normal Range Dental / Surgical Cutoff
Hemoglobin (Hb) Men: 13.5–17.5 g/dL
Women: 12.0–15.5 g/dL		 Elective surgery safe: > 10.0 g/dL
Platelet Count 150,000 – 450,000 /mm³ Safe minor oral surgery: > 50,000 /mm³
Platelet transfusion if < 40,000 /mm³
Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) 1,500 – 8,000 /mm³ If < 1,000 /mm³: defer elective dental care; antibiotic cover mandatory
Prothrombin Time / INR PT: 11–13.5 sec
INR: 0.8 – 1.1		 Minor dental surgery safe: INR ≤ 2.5 – 3.0 (with local hemostatics)
Bleeding Time (Ivy) 2 – 7 minutes Abnormal: > 10 mins (suspect platelet dysfunction/vWD)
Clotting Time (Lee-White) 5 – 11 minutes Abnormal: > 15 mins (suspect coagulation factor deficiency)
Fasting Blood Glucose 70 – 99 mg/dL Impaired: 100–125 mg/dL
Diabetes: ≥ 126 mg/dL
HbA1c (Glycated Hb) Normal: < 5.7% Well controlled: < 7.0%
Poor control (> 8.5%): higher infection & delayed healing risk
Blood Pressure (JNC-7) Normal: < 120 / 80 mmHg Stage 1: 140–159 / 90–99 mmHg
Stage 2: ≥ 160 / ≥ 100 mmHg (limit epinephrine)
