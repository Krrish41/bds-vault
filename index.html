Final Year Specialty 01

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery (OMFS)

Exodontia, impactions (Winter & Pell-Gregory), fractures (Le Fort, mandible), orthognathic surgery, TMJ ankylosis, and maxillectomy.

Final Year Specialty 02

Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics

Carious lesion preparation, amalgam & composite restorations, root canal anatomy, instrumentation, obturation, and periapical surgery.

Final Year Specialty 03

Prosthodontics & Crown-Bridge

Complete dentures, removable partial dentures (Kennedy classification), fixed partial dentures, abutment selection, and dental implants.

Final Year Specialty 04

Periodontology & Implantology

Periodontal anatomy, pocket depth dynamics, bone defects, flap surgeries, regenerative procedures (GTR), and mucogingival esthetics.

Final Year Specialty 05

Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics

Angle's classification of malocclusion, cephalometric analysis (Steiner, Tweed), myofunctional appliances, and fixed mechanotherapy.

Final Year Specialty 06

Pediatric & Preventive Dentistry

Primary tooth morphology, behavior management (Frankl scale), space maintainers, pulpotomy/pulpectomy, and pit & fissure sealants.

Final Year Specialty 07

Oral Medicine & Radiology (OMR)

Radiographic projection techniques (IOPA, OPG, CBCT), radiation physics, systemic mucosal disorders, and temporomandibular joint imaging.

Final Year Specialty 08

Public Health Dentistry (PHD)

Epidemiological indices (DMFT, CPITN), fluoridation programs, health administration, biostatistics, and national health policies.

Normal Clinical Laboratory Values (Chairside Reference)

Parameter Standard Normal Range Dental / Surgical Cutoff
Hemoglobin (Hb) Men: 13.5-17.5 g/dL
Women: 12.0-15.5 g/dL		 Elective surgery safe: > 10.0 g/dL
Platelet Count 150,000 – 450,000 /mm³ Safe minor oral surgery: > 50,000 /mm³
Platelet transfusion if < 40,000 /mm³
Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) 1,500 – 8,000 /mm³ If < 1,000 /mm³: defer elective dental care; antibiotic cover mandatory
Prothrombin Time / INR PT: 11-13.5 sec
INR: 0.8 – 1.1		 Minor dental surgery safe: INR ≤ 2.5 - 3.0 (with local hemostatics)
Bleeding Time (Ivy) 2 – 7 minutes Abnormal: > 10 mins (suspect platelet dysfunction/vWD)
Clotting Time (Lee-White) 5 – 11 minutes Abnormal: > 15 mins (suspect coagulation factor deficiency)
Fasting Blood Glucose 70 – 99 mg/dL Impaired: 100-125 mg/dL
Diabetes: ≥ 126 mg/dL
HbA1c (Glycated Hb) Normal: < 5.7% Well controlled: < 7.0%
Poor control (> 8.5%): higher infection & delayed healing risk
Blood Pressure (JNC-7) Normal: < 120 / 80 mmHg Stage 1: 140-159 / 90-99 mmHg
Stage 2: ≥ 160 / ≥ 100 mmHg (limit epinephrine)
